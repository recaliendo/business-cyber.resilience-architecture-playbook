# Incident response policy

**Owner:** ______  **Approved:** ______  **Next review:** ______

1. Anyone who suspects an incident reports it to ______ immediately, by ______.
2. The incident lead follows the [incident runbook](../incident-runbook.md): identify, contain, communicate, fix, record.
3. Severity levels are SEV1 (data exposure or full outage), SEV2 (major feature down or suspected compromise) and SEV3 (degraded service).
4. Any possible exposure of personal data triggers the breach-notification worksheet and a decision made with counsel. We never conclude "no notification needed" without that review.
5. Every SEV1 and SEV2 gets a written incident note within 5 business days.
6. The runbook is rehearsed (a tabletop exercise) at least once a year.
