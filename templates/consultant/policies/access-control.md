# Access control policy

**Owner:** ______  **Approved:** ______  **Next review:** ______

1. **Named accounts only.** No shared logins. Everyone uses a password manager.
2. **MFA wherever it is available,** preferably passkeys or hardware keys. Required on hosting/cloud, source control, the domain registrar and DNS, email/identity, Apple Developer, payments, and any system holding customer data.
3. **Least privilege.** Admin roles are granted only when needed, recorded in the access register with a reason, and kept separate from daily-use accounts where the vendor allows.
4. **Granting and revoking.** ______ grants access on request. Leavers lose access the same day, following the offboarding checklist.
5. **Quarterly access review** of every critical system, recorded with the date and any changes made.
6. **Recovery.** Recovery codes are stored in the password manager, and the recovery email and phone are themselves protected by MFA.

*Solo founder note:* one person holds every role. Record this plainly. Reviews are self-reviews until a second person joins.
