# Vendor management policy

**Owner:** ______  **Approved:** ______  **Next review:** ______

1. Before a new vendor receives customer data, we add it to the data and vendor inventory with its purpose, regions and data classes.
2. We sign or accept a DPA (data processing agreement) with every vendor that processes personal data.
3. For critical vendors, we review their assurance report (SOC 2, ISO 27001 or equivalent): scope, period, exceptions, any bridge letter, and the customer responsibilities the report assigns to us.
4. A vendor's certification does not make us compliant. We carry out the responsibilities the vendor leaves to us.
5. When a vendor notifies us of a subprocessor change, we review it.
6. We review the inventory every year, removing unused vendors and revoking their access.
